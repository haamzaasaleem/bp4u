    Design tips for embroidery

    Embroidery is stitched right into the fabric. For the best results:

    • Keep it simple. Tiny details don’t stitch well.
    • Use bold text, at least 0.25″ tall. Thin lines are hard to read in thread.
    • Use solid colors. Gradients and photos can’t be embroidered.
    • Relax, we’ve got you. Our team digitizes your logo and sends a free proof before we stitch.
