Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum order?

Nope! We print and embroider from 1 piece to 5,000. Bulk pricing kicks in at 12 pieces and gets better at 24, 48, and 96+.

Can you embroider individual names on each jacket?

Yes! Personalized names and credentials are our specialty. Just send us a list with each person’s name, title, and size, and we’ll match everything up.

What file types can I upload for my logo?

PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF, or AI files all work. Don’t have a clean file? Send what you have and our team will recreate it, free on most orders.

How long does an order take?

Most orders are ready in about 7–10 business days after you approve your proof. Need it sooner? Call us and we’ll do our best to make it happen.

Do you print business cards and marketing materials?

Yes! Business cards, postcards, door hangers, flyers, and more. Call or text (559) 474-2808 for the best pricing.

Do you also build websites and do marketing?

We do! Shawn has 22+ years in digital media and advertising. We offer website design, search engine optimization (SEO), and digital marketing to help your business get found and grow.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Madera and proudly serve Madera, Clovis, Fresno, and communities throughout Central California.

Should I choose embroidery or printing?

Embroidery looks professional and lasts on jackets, polos, and hats. Screen printing is ideal for t-shirts, larger designs, and bigger quantities. Not sure? We’ll recommend the best fit.
