Product Photo Setup

Open Design Studio

How it works

  1. Pick the product, color, and side.
  2. Load the photo (front-facing, plain background works best).
  3. Drag the green box over where logos can go and enter its real width.
  4. Download photo and save it to the folder shown.
  5. Save side, repeat for other sides/colors, then copy the settings into js/product-photos.js.
